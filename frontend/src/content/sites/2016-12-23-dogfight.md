In 1983 I had a BBC Micro computer. One of my favourite games was [Dogfight](https://bbcmicro.co.uk/game.php?id=26).

I decided to recreate it in JavaScript.

## Controls

### White plane

- left cursor - nose down
- right cursor - nose up
- space - fire

### Red plane

- Q - nose down
- W - nose up
- A - fire
